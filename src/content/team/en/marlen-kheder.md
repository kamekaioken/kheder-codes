I have spent twelve years building software other people have to work in — first
employed, these days freelance. The common thread is line-of-business
applications: many users, a long life ahead of them, and a domain you have to
understand before you can put it into code.

## What I work on

I cut systems into shape before they are built, and then help build them:
frontend, backend, infrastructure. At DonnaDesk I own the platform for voice AI
agents — real-time audio over LiveKit, a runtime on Bun and Elysia, infrastructure
as code with Pulumi.

## What I like working with

TypeScript on both ends. C# and .NET when that is where a client is at home.
Monorepos as soon as more than one team shares a product — and one dependency
fewer rather than one too many.
