I have spent twelve years building software other people have to work in — first
employed, these days freelance. The common thread is line-of-business
applications: reinsurance, tax consulting, international cooperation. Systems
with many users, a long life ahead of them, and a domain you have to understand
before you can put it into code.

## What I work on

At DonnaDesk I own the platform for voice AI agents: real-time audio over
LiveKit, a runtime on Bun and Elysia, infrastructure as code with Pulumi.
Alongside that I take client projects from the first sketch to running them in
production.

## What I like working with

TypeScript on both ends. C# and .NET when that is where a client is at home.
Monorepos as soon as more than one team shares a product — and one dependency
fewer rather than one too many.
